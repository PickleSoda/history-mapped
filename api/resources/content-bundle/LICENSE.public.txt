# Licensing of this content bundle

This bundle was exported from History Mapped, an interactive historical atlas.

- Facts derived from Wikidata are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
- Prose derived from Wikipedia is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution is given per record through its `source_citations`.
- Geometry derived from OpenHistoricalMap is released under CC0 1.0.
- Images are referenced as Wikimedia Commons links together with their credits. No image files are copied into the bundle.

Reuse of a record must keep the licence that applies to the parts it contains.
